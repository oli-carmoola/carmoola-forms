COMPLAINT ACTIONS · GETTING STARTED

Initial Customer Call

Before you start investigating, make an initial attempt to speak with the customer and record what happened. This helps set expectations and makes the process feel more personal.

1. Make the introduction call

Call the customer to introduce yourself as their complaint handler. You can adapt the suggestions below to sound natural.

What to coverIntroduce yourself and confirm that you will be investigating their complaint. Let them know they can contact you if anything comes up, and that you will reach out when you need further information. Reassure them that a period without updates does not mean their complaint has been forgotten.
▾   Suggested call script & talking points

“Hi, is that [customer]? It’s [your name] calling from Carmoola. I’m going to be looking after your complaint, so I just wanted to introduce myself properly.

I’ll be reviewing everything and looking into the concerns you’ve raised. If you have any questions or there’s anything you’d like to add, you can reach out to me. Otherwise, don’t worry — I’ll get in touch if I need anything from you.

I’m looking after a number of complaints, so there may be periods when you don’t hear from me while I’m gathering information. That doesn’t mean I’ve stopped working on yours. I’ll keep you updated as things progress, and we’ll make sure you receive a response within the applicable timeframe.”

Before ending: Ask if there is anything else they would like you to consider, check their preferred method of contact and avoid promising a particular outcome.

2. Record the outcome

Choose what happened when you tried to contact the customer.
